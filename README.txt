AZAGOHRATH — AUTHORSHIP PROOF BUNDLE

PURPOSE
-------
This bundle contains cryptographic provenance material.

It may contain:
- authorship records
- SHA-256 references
- provenance registry
- master index
- timestamp manifests
- OpenTimestamps proof files

IMPORTANT
---------
A cryptographic hash demonstrates integrity of specific bytes.
A digital signature can demonstrate control of a signing key.

These mechanisms do not by themselves establish legal copyright,
identity, priority over every competing claim, or authorship of
material not cryptographically linked to the evidence.

PRIVATE KEYS
------------
No private signing key is intentionally included in this bundle.

SHA-256 VERIFICATION
--------------------
On macOS:

shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt

OPEN TIMESTAMPS
---------------
For .ots files, verification may require the OpenTimestamps client
and network access.

END
